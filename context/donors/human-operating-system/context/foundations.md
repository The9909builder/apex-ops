## The Four Roots

### 1. Biological Root: Autopoiesis and the Free Energy Principle

- The organism is an **autopoietic system** (Maturana & Varela): a self-referential
  network of processes that continuously produces the components required to
  maintain its own identity. Operationally: the "internal factory" whose production
  must match component decay, or the system disintegrates.
- The system is bounded by a **Markov blanket**: a statistical partition of
  sensory states (inputs) and active states (outputs). Everything the environment
  knows about the system, and the system about the environment, passes through it.
- **Free Energy Principle** (Friston): the primary objective is minimizing surprise
  (variational free energy) by maintaining a generative model that predicts
  environmental perturbations. Operationally: anxiety is sustained prediction
  error; model updates are the treatment, willpower is not.

### 2. Structural Root: The Nature of Order and Wholeness

- **Centers** (Alexander): localized zones of coherence intensified by their
  surroundings. A center is an active field effect, not a static part.
- **Wholeness** is an objective, structural degree of life achieved when centers
  interlock. Operationally: each center is a stock of life-energy augmented or
  depleted by the structural configuration of neighboring centers.
- The 15 properties (see fifteen-properties.md) are the recurring geometry of
  living structure across all scales.

### 3. Dynamic Root: Systems Thinking and Feedback

- **Stocks and flows** (Meadows): accumulations and their rates of change.
- **Balancing loops** maintain homeostasis by correcting discrepancies between
  current state and goal.
- **Reinforcing loops** drive exponential growth or decay; obsession, fear, and
  busyness are reinforcing loops wearing different clothes.
- **Leverage points** are ordered: numbers < information flows < rules < paradigm.
  The strongest interventions are the least visible.

### 4. Philosophical Root: Finitude and Epistemology

- **Temporal finitude** (Burkeman): time is a non-renewable time-stock, fixed at
  roughly 4,000 weeks. Operationally: the efficiency paradox. More efficiency
  drains the stock faster because saved time is reabsorbed by new demands.
- **Middle Way epistemology** (Ellis): avoid absolutisation of beliefs; maintain
  provisionality; integrate conflicting desires rather than choosing dogmatically.
  Operationally: every belief ships with its revision condition.

## Claims Ledger (provisionality in practice)

- "Wholeness is an objective, measurable degree of life": held as a provisional
  model, an archetypal meaning used for orientation, subject to skeptical scrutiny.
- "Minimizing free energy is the universal objective of living systems": held as a
  functional protocol, not a metaphysical truth.

Both claims stay in the ledger precisely so the system can revise them without
identity collapse. This is the Middle Way applied to HOS itself.
