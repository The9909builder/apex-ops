## The 10 Volumes (Data Libraries)

Each volume encodes semiotic protocols that map external perturbations into
internal predictive models. Use them as lookup libraries during Orientation and
Decision; use the failure modes as diagnostic checklists.

| # | Volume | Encodes | Use when | Failure mode |
|---|--------|---------|----------|--------------|
| I | Biological Substrate | Autopoietic mechanics; the body as internal factory; component production must match decay | energy, health, metabolic stocks decline | Physical disintegration or burnout from neglected metabolic stocks |
| II | Cognitive Interface | Predictive processing; Markov blanket mediation; the language of thought | chronic anxiety, thrashing, model feels stale | Systemic surprise leads to chronic anxiety and model collapse |
| III | Architecture of Wholeness | The 15 properties (see fifteen-properties.md) | designing or repairing structure at any scale | Dead, fragmented, mechanically rigid arrangements |
| IV | Systems Dynamics | Stocks, flows, oscillations; the spruce budworm lesson | problems that return bigger after each fix | Linear absolute thinking amplifies the problem it targets |
| V | Interactional Pragmatics | Watzlawick's axioms; communication as reciprocal perturbation | conflict, isolation, communication breakdown | Failure of structural coupling leading to system isolation |
| VI | Formal Axiology | Hierarchical valuation of wholeness; the logic of value | misaligned goals, low-value busyness | High-value stocks depleted in favor of low-value flows |
| VII | Philosophy of Craft | Technê; the intelligence of the hand; making heals the maker | alienation, meaning-depletion, all-talk work | Meaning-depletion from lack of physical engagement with reality |
| VIII | Network Topology | Information movement through thick boundaries | overwhelm, noise flooding, identity thinning | Boundary thinning lets noise destroy identity |
| IX | Temporal Finitude | The 4,000 weeks protocol; the efficiency paradox | busyness despite productivity gains | Terminal time-stock depletion; the reinforcing loop of chronic busyness |
| X | The Middle Way | Equilibrium and integration of desire, belief, meaning | oscillation, dogmatism, extremism | Systemic oscillation from shooting past equilibrium into extreme dogmas |

## Diagnostic Routing

Symptom to volume, fastest path:

- Burnout or health decline: Volume I, then IV (what loop drains the stock?)
- Anxiety without a specific threat: Volume II, run Loop 1
- Structure feels dead or fragmented: Volume III, audit against the 15 properties
- Fix that made it worse: Volume IV, check loop type at the leverage point
- Relationship or team breakdown: Volume V, restore structural coupling
- Busy but empty: Volume VI, then IX; run the value hierarchy and time-stock audit
- Work feels meaningless: Volume VII, find the craft engagement
- Overwhelm from inputs: Volume VIII, rebuild thick boundaries
- Oscillating between extremes: Volume X, apply provisionality and integration

## The Gap HOS Fills

Meadows and Ellis identify the historical absence of epistemology and psychology
in systems theory. HOS closes it by running predictive processing (psychology)
and the Middle Way (epistemology) in the engine room, so the observer is part of
the system being observed, not outside it.
