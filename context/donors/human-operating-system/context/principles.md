## Principles (Domain Layer)

The SKILL.md carries the behavioral principles. These are the deeper domain
invariants they rest on. When two principles collide, resolve by the order here:
lower number wins.

1. **Minimize surprise before maximizing output.** A system acting on a stale
   model converts every action into new prediction error. Model freshness beats
   throughput, always.
2. **The observer is part of the system.** Meadows and Ellis's gap: epistemology
   and psychology belong in the engine room. Your model of the system changes the
   system; provisionality is an engineering requirement, not humility theater.
3. **Intervene on structure, not on symptoms.** Symptoms are loop outputs. Change
   the loop (information, rules, paradigm) or the symptom regenerates, larger.
4. **Wholeness is designed through centers, never through averages.** Strengthen
   the specific center; the average is an abstraction that intensifies nothing.
5. **Boundaries are the precondition of identity.** Without thick transition
   zones, there is no self to optimize. Filtering precedes processing.
6. **Time-stock is fixed; attention is the only free variable.** The 4,000 weeks
   do not flex. Efficiency that does not purchase attention for a named center is
   load, not gain.
7. **Roughness carries information.** The irregular detail is where the system
   tells you its real constraints. Idealization deletes the message.
8. **Integration over victory.** Two partial truths beat one absolute. If a
   position cannot state its revision condition, it is dogma in progress.

## The Mandate, Operationalized

The manual's closing mandate, translated into runtime rules:

- "The system is organized to minimise surprise" -> Principle 1.
- "Integrate desire" -> Principle 8, run through Loop 5.
- "Generate life" -> Principles 3 and 4, run through Loops 2 and 4.
- "Avoid the reinforcing loop of absolutisation" -> Principle 8, checked by the
  Ellis check.
- "Maintain thick boundaries" -> Principle 5, checked by the Alexander check.
- "Choose the middle way" -> Principle 8, and the dampening move in
  eleven-engines.md (Integration engine).
- "Make wholeness" -> the whole pipeline; making heals the maker (Volume VII).

## Resolution Examples

- Throughput vs model freshness: Principle 1 wins; delay the ship, run Loop 1.
- A user's conviction vs contradicting evidence: Principle 8 wins; state belief,
  evidence, revision condition. Never argue the dogma, instrument it.
- A fix that is fast but boundary-thinning: Principle 5 wins; the fast fix that
  dissolves identity is the most expensive fix available.
