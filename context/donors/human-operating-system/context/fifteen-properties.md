## The 15 Properties of Living Structure

Each property: definition, operational test, application move. Phase 4 (Action)
must name at least one property explicitly; this file is its menu.

| # | Property | Definition | Operational test | Application move |
|---|----------|-----------|------------------|------------------|
| 1 | Levels of Scale | Nested hierarchies; each center roughly half or twice its neighbors | Is there a coherent jump in scale between levels, or an undifferentiated mush? | Engineer nested hierarchies for robust field effects |
| 2 | Strong Centers | Field effects applied recursively; smaller centers nested inside a dominant one | Can you point to the singular point of coherence? | Nest smaller centers to generate one dominant coherence |
| 3 | Boundaries | Thick protective transition zones intensifying the inner center | Is the transition abrupt and thin, or graduated and thick? | Build thick zones that mediate connectivity while protecting the core |
| 4 | Alternating Repetition | Recurring elements modulated with subtle variation | Do repetitions vary, or are they stamped clones? | Modulate recurrences to local conditions; kill mechanical rigidity |
| 5 | Positive Space | No leftover zones; every segment is a coherent center | Is there dead space nobody owns or uses? | Make every spatial or temporal segment function as a center |
| 6 | Good Shape | Whole synthesized from intense, self-contained centers | Do the parts have their own integrity, or exist only as fragments of the whole? | Build self-contained centers, then synthesize |
| 7 | Local Symmetries | Interlocking symmetrical segments binding parts into wholes | Are aligned segments locked together, or floating separately? | Overlap symmetries to act as structural glue |
| 8 | Deep Interlock and Ambiguity | Transition zones belonging to both system and surroundings | Is there a zone both sides claim? | Interpenetrate system and environment through shared transition zones |
| 9 | Contrast | Sharpened distinctions between opposing qualities | Are oppositions crisp or smeared? | Sharpen the distinction to intensify the focal center's character |
| 10 | Gradients | Gradual variation in size or quality along a direction | Are transitions stepped or smooth where they should adapt? | Deploy gradual variation to respond adaptively to shifts |
| 11 | Roughness | Local irregularities as functional perfection | Are irregularities treated as information or as defects? | Incorporate constraints and irregularities; refuse abstract idealization |
| 12 | Echoes | Underlying structural similarity across scales | Does the deep pattern repeat recognizably at every scale? | Embed the same generative pattern at every level |
| 13 | The Void | Empty centers of calm anchoring active regions | Is there defended calm, or is everything allocated? | Preserve empty centers; slack is load-bearing structure |
| 14 | Simplicity and Inner Calm | Excised unnecessary complexity | What can be removed without losing life? | Excise until only the functional remains; majesty through subtraction |
| 15 | Not-Separateness | Boundaries fragmented until system melts into environment | Does the system fight or feed its context? | Fragment the boundary until total structural integration |

## Use in the Pipeline

- **Loop 2 (Orientation):** properties 2, 5, 13 diagnose which centers are alive.
- **Loop 3 (Decision):** property 8 informs whether the leverage point sits inside
  a boundary problem; property 1 prevents scale-mismatched choices.
- **Loop 4 (Action):** the default set for interventions is 3 (Boundaries),
  13 (Void), 11 (Roughness), 4 (Alternating Repetition). Name what you apply.
- **Loop 5 (Integration):** property 11 licenses keeping the result's roughness;
  property 14 asks what to remove next cycle.

## Anti-Pattern Signature

A structure failing most of the 15 properties reads as: stamped uniformity
(fails 4), dead leftover zones (fails 5), thin abrupt boundaries (fails 3),
no defended calm (fails 13), parts with no independent integrity (fails 6).
That cluster is the smell of a system organized by abstraction rather than by
wholeness.
